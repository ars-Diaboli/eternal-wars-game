Q2: MAP-10 Buff stacking.** 
Can a hero visit several morale or luck buffs before a battle - yes player can visit several 
and how do they add up (within the ±5 cap)? - buffs stack up to ±5 cap;
Does the Temple's +3 depend on the calendar day (day 7 of the week) or on the week's last day? - day 7 of the week;
Do visits give the buff to the hero only, or to heroes that meet later? - hero only;
Do buffs end on a battle against neutrals as well? - any battle (in town siege, creature bank, abandoned mine, neutral guards etc)
