# Creature icon prompts: Conflux

The Conflux portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Sheets C1–C3 hold two creature lines each, the bases in the top row
and their upgrades directly below them (a spirit of tier 2 becomes an elemental of tier 3, ADR-320); C4 holds the wisp, which has no
upgrade, and second takes. The output rules and the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative
prompt is [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

## Portraits

**Sheet C1** · 1024 × 1024 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Strong directional light from the top left with crisp highlights and a faint cool
rim light, deep rich shadows and high contrast, a full range of values from near-black to bright highlights, rich deep natural
colours, vivid but never garish, never faded or washed out. Each portrait shows the creature from the chest up in three-quarter view
facing right, head and weapon inside the middle of the cell; small creatures and beasts show the whole body, giant creatures and
dragons only the head and neck. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no drawn outline.
Not a photograph, not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Conflux creatures: elemental beings, the Wheel's ancient guardians, each a
figure made of its own element with a crystal glowing at its heart in the element's colour, a few fragments of weathered stone and old
green bronze floating close around it. Almost no faces: never a human face, at most two faint glowing hollows on a smooth or rough
head; some have a roughly human-shaped body with shoulders and arms; none wear cloth or armour. 4 separate icons arranged in a grid of
2 columns and 2 rows, each alone in its own equal square cell, centred with about 10 % empty margin, all at the same scale and with
the same light; nothing crosses into another cell. No grid lines, no borders, no frames, no text, no labels, no numbers. Transparent
background, PNG with alpha. The icons, top row left to right, then bottom row: 1 a translucent whirlwind of pale blue-white air, a
broad humanoid chest and shoulders with long flowing arms of wind, its body below the chest narrowing into a spinning funnel, a
rounded head of swirling cloud with no face, dust and leaves caught in the wind; 2 a figure of clear deep-blue water rising from a
swirling wave instead of legs, broad rounded shoulders and heavy arms of flowing water, a smooth domed head with no face, only two
faint pale glints, foam and spray at its edges; 3 the same creature as the one above it, clearly upgraded and told apart at a glance:
the same shape and build but larger, more powerful and richer in colour: its whirlwind now dark blue-grey storm cloud, white lightning
crackling through its body and arms, rain streaming off it, a darker and heavier funnel; 4 the same creature as the one above it,
clearly upgraded and told apart at a glance: the same shape and build but larger, more powerful and richer in colour: frozen into
jagged translucent blue-white ice, sharp ice crystals jutting from the shoulders and back, arms ending in ice shards, frost mist
curling off it, a cold pale-blue glow inside
```
