# Creature icon prompts: Conflux

The Conflux portraits. The shared blocks (`CREATURE-STYLE`, `CREATURE-NEG`, `BACKDROP-STYLE`), the output rules and the checks are in
[creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); `ICON-SHEET-4`, `ICON-SHEET-6` and `ICON-NEG` are in
[icon-prompts.md](../icon-prompts.md#blocks). Cells as listed.

## Blocks

**`CREATURE-conflux`** (look, second in every prompt of this file):

```text
Conflux creatures: elemental spirits bound to the Wheel's mechanism, each a figure made of its own element, fragments of ancient
stone and old green bronze rune bands floating close around it.
```

**`BACKDROP-conflux`** (the backdrop, after `BACKDROP-STYLE`; generated alone at 1024 × 1024 as `creature_backdrop_conflux.png`):

```text
a ring of ancient standing stones with old bronze gears half-buried, a soft shimmer in the air
```
