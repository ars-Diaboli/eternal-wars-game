# Creature icon prompts: Conflux

The Conflux portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Each sheet holds single creatures, one a cell. The output rules and the
checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative prompt is
[`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

## Portraits

**Sheet C1** · 1536 × 1024 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Naturalistic light from the top left with soft shadows and a faint cool fill
light, deep shadows and a full range of values, muted natural colours. Each portrait shows the creature from the chest up in
three-quarter view facing right, head and weapon inside the middle of the cell; small creatures and beasts show the whole body, giant
creatures and dragons only the head and neck. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no
drawn outline. Not a photograph, not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Conflux creatures: elemental spirits bound to the Wheel's
mechanism, each a figure made of its own element, fragments of ancient stone and old green bronze rune bands floating close around it.
6 separate icons arranged in a grid of 3 columns and 2 rows, each alone in its own equal square cell, centred with about 10 % empty
margin, all at the same scale and with the same light; nothing crosses into another cell. No grid lines, no borders, no frames, no
text, no labels, no numbers. Transparent background, PNG with alpha. The icons, top row left to right, then bottom row: 1 a small
floating orb of soft white light, a few bronze rune fragments circling it; 2 a hulking figure of rough grey stone and packed earth,
roots and crystals in its body, a bronze rune plate in its chest; 3 a swirling figure of pale grey wind and cloud with a vague face,
dust and leaves caught in it; 4 a figure of roaring orange fire over a charred black core, bronze bands around its arms; 5 a figure of
clear blue-green water with foam crests, a face in the wave; 6 a heavy figure of black cooling rock with glowing orange magma between
the plates
```
