# Creature icon prompts: Conflux

The Conflux portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Sheets C1–C6 hold one creature line each, the base on the left and its
upgrade on the right (a spirit of tier 2 becomes an elemental of tier 3, ADR-320); C7 holds the wisp and its upgrade, the worldwatcher
(tier 2, ADR-364). The output rules and the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative prompt
is [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

## Portraits

**Sheet C1** · 1024 × 768 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Strong directional light from the top left with crisp highlights and a faint cool
rim light, deep rich shadows and high contrast, a full range of values from near-black to bright highlights, rich deep natural
colours, vivid but never garish, never faded or washed out. Each portrait shows the whole creature at full height, from the top of the
head (or wings) down to the feet, a rider together with the whole mount down to its hooves, beasts, giant creatures and dragons whole
as well, in three-quarter view facing right; never cropped at the chest, waist or knees, every figure on the sheet framed the same
full-length way. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no drawn outline. Not a photograph,
not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Conflux creatures: elemental beings, the Wheel's ancient guardians, each a figure made of its
own element with a crystal glowing at its heart in the element's colour, a few fragments of weathered stone and old green bronze
floating close around it. Almost no faces: never a human face, at most two faint glowing hollows on a smooth or rough head; some have
a roughly human-shaped body with shoulders and arms; none wear cloth or armour. 2 separate icons side by side in one row, each alone
in its own equal upright cell 2 wide by 3 tall, the whole creature fitted inside its cell from the top of the head to the feet with
about 8 % empty margin, a long or wide creature (a rider, a beast, a dragon) drawn smaller so that its whole length fits the cell's
width, both at the same scale and with the same light; nothing crosses into the other cell. No grid lines, no borders, no frames, no
text, no labels, no numbers. Transparent background, PNG with alpha. The icons, left to right: 1 an air spirit, small and slender, the
smallest figure on the sheet: a thin, mostly see-through spiralling funnel of nearly colourless white and silver air, a narrow upper
body with thin wispy arms, a small round head of swirling mist with no face, much empty space between its streaks of wind, dust and
dry leaves caught in it, a white crystal; 2 the same creature as the one to its left, clearly upgraded and told apart at a glance:
much larger and heavier: a storm elemental, a towering top-heavy thundercloud spreading wide at the shoulders like an anvil cloud,
dense dark charcoal and slate-purple storm cloud, opaque and dark, not white and not pale blue, bright yellow-white lightning forking
through its arms and chest, rain streaming down in sheets below it, a yellow crystal
```
