# Creature icon prompts: Inferno

The Inferno portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Each sheet holds one creature line, the base on the left and its
upgrade on the right. The output rules and the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative
prompt is [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

Sheet I10 holds the gog and the magog, and sheet I11 the breeder and the fel breeder: Inferno creatures by lore, not hired in the town
(neutral in the game), drawn in its style and on its backdrop; their files keep their neutral ids.

## Portraits

**Sheet I1** · 1024 × 768 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Strong directional light from the top left with crisp highlights and a faint cool
rim light, deep rich shadows and high contrast, a full range of values from near-black to bright highlights, rich deep natural
colours, vivid but never garish, never faded or washed out. Each portrait shows the whole creature at full height, from the top of the
head (or wings) down to the feet, a rider together with the whole mount down to its hooves, beasts, giant creatures and dragons whole
as well, in three-quarter view facing right; never cropped at the chest, waist or knees, every figure on the sheet framed the same
full-length way. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no drawn outline. Not a photograph,
not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Inferno creatures: demons born from the ash of burned cities, obsidian-black and ash-grey skin
cracked with seams glowing red like lava, natural orange fire, blackened bronze, horns and smoke; hungry and cruel. 2 separate icons
side by side in one row, each alone in its own equal upright cell 2 wide by 3 tall, the whole creature fitted inside its cell from the
top of the head to the feet with about 8 % empty margin, a long or wide creature (a rider, a beast, a dragon) drawn smaller so that
its whole length fits the cell's width, both at the same scale and with the same light; nothing crosses into the other cell. No grid
lines, no borders, no frames, no text, no labels, no numbers. Transparent background, PNG with alpha. The icons, left to right: 1 a
small red-skinned imp, a lesser demon shown whole, drawn with believable anatomy and natural proportions like a small lean
human-shaped creature, never a cartoon or caricature: the head in proportion to the body, not oversized, a small slight compact body,
short in the torso, standing only about three quarters of the cell's height, lean and sinewy with a flat hard stomach and visible
ribs, no pot belly, wiry but properly muscled arms and legs of believable length, a narrow gaunt face with high cheekbones, a hooked
nose and narrowed cruel eyes, long pointed ears swept back, two short sharp horns, lips drawn back over small sharp teeth in a cold
sneer, not a wide grin, large leathery bat wings, big for its small body, raised and half spread above its shoulders, each wing as
long as the imp's torso and legs together, the wing tips rising above its head, a long thin tail ending in an arrowhead barb, clawed
hands and feet, empty-handed, standing in a tense crouch; 2 the same creature as the one to its left, clearly upgraded and told apart
at a glance: the same face and build but larger, heavier armed, more ornate and richer in colour, with the same natural proportions,
the same small compact body and the same large wings, no pot belly: darker skin with ember cracks, longer horns, a blackened bronze
collar, a long three-pronged trident of blackened bronze, its shaft taller than the imp itself, held upright in its claws; no glow, no
haze, no mist and no mana around the trident
```
