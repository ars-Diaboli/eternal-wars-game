# Creature icon prompts: Inferno

The Inferno portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Each sheet holds two creatures in its top row and their upgrades
directly below them. The output rules and the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative
prompt is [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

## Portraits

**Sheet I1** · 1024 × 1024 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Naturalistic light from the top left with soft shadows and a faint cool fill
light, deep shadows and a full range of values, muted natural colours. Each portrait shows the creature from the chest up in
three-quarter view facing right, head and weapon inside the middle of the cell; small creatures and beasts show the whole body, giant
creatures and dragons only the head and neck. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no
drawn outline. Not a photograph, not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Inferno creatures: demons born from the ash of burned cities,
obsidian-black and ash-grey skin cracked with seams glowing red like lava, natural orange fire, blackened bronze, horns and smoke;
hungry and cruel. 4 separate icons arranged in a grid of 2 columns and 2 rows, each alone in its own equal square cell, centred with
about 10 % empty margin, all at the same scale and with the same light; nothing crosses into another cell. No grid lines, no borders,
no frames, no text, no labels, no numbers. Transparent background, PNG with alpha. The icons, top row left to right, then bottom row:
1 a small red-skinned imp with bat wings, a long thin tail and a wide grin, a glowing coal in its claws, the whole body shown; 2 a
muscular ash-grey demon with curved ram horns and hooves, a jagged cleaver of blackened bronze; 3 the same creature as the one above
it, upgraded, bigger and more dangerous: darker skin with ember cracks, small horns, a blackened bronze collar, a wisp of blue mana
pulled into its claws; 4 the same creature as the one above it, upgraded, bigger and more dangerous: larger horns ringed with bronze,
a harness of spikes, a whip of chains
```
