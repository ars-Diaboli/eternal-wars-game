# Creature icon prompts: Dungeon

The Dungeon portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Each sheet holds three creatures in its top row and their upgrades
directly below them. The output rules and the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative
prompt is [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

## Portraits

**Sheet D1** · 1536 × 1024 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Naturalistic light from the top left with soft shadows and a faint cool fill
light, deep shadows and a full range of values, muted natural colours. Each portrait shows the creature from the chest up in
three-quarter view facing right, head and weapon inside the middle of the cell; small creatures and beasts show the whole body, giant
creatures and dragons only the head and neck. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no
drawn outline. Not a photograph, not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Dungeon creatures: dark elves and lightless beasts of the
deep, ash-grey and black skin, black and dark-violet armour with thin silver inlay, a faint violet glow; cold, elegant, patient. 6
separate icons arranged in a grid of 3 columns and 2 rows, each alone in its own equal square cell, centred with about 10 % empty
margin, all at the same scale and with the same light; nothing crosses into another cell. No grid lines, no borders, no frames, no
text, no labels, no numbers. Transparent background, PNG with alpha. The icons, top row left to right, then bottom row: 1 a pale
eyeless cave dweller with grey skin and a lizard-like face, a crude stone-tipped spear; 2 a dark-elf scout with ash-grey skin and white
hair under a black hood, two curved daggers; 3 a medusa with green scales, hair of small snakes and a long serpent tail, a curved
silver dagger, eyes glowing faint violet; 4 the same creature as the one above it, upgraded, bigger and more dangerous: violet painted
scars, a bone helmet, a spear in each hand; 5 the same creature as the one above it, upgraded, bigger and more dangerous: a black mask
over the lower face, violet-edged blades, a cloak melting into shadow; 6 the same creature as the one above it, upgraded, bigger and
more dangerous: pale diseased scales, the hair-snakes dripping venom, a ragged black veil
```
