# Creature icon prompts: Dungeon

The Dungeon portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Each sheet holds two creatures in its top row and their upgrades
directly below them. The output rules and the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative
prompt is [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

## Portraits

**Sheet D1** · 1024 × 1024 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Strong directional light from the top left with crisp highlights and a faint cool
rim light, deep rich shadows and high contrast, a full range of values from near-black to bright highlights, rich deep natural
colours, vivid but never garish, never faded or washed out. Each portrait shows the creature from the chest up in three-quarter view
facing right, head and weapon inside the middle of the cell; small creatures and beasts show the whole body, giant creatures and
dragons only the head and neck. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no drawn outline.
Not a photograph, not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Dungeon creatures: dark elves and lightless beasts of the deep, ash-grey and
black skin, black and dark-violet armour with thin silver inlay, a faint violet glow, touches of cyan bioluminescence; cold, elegant,
patient. 4 separate icons arranged in a grid of 2 columns and 2 rows, each alone in its own equal square cell, centred with about 10 %
empty margin, all at the same scale and with the same light; nothing crosses into another cell. No grid lines, no borders, no frames,
no text, no labels, no numbers. Transparent background, PNG with alpha. The icons, top row left to right, then bottom row: 1 a pale
troglodyte, a small hunched reptile-like cave beast shown whole, crouching on bent clawed legs, not a man: a smooth eyeless head with
no eyes at all, a long lizard snout and a huge wide jaw of needle teeth, a ridge of spines from the skull down the back, long thin
arms with clawed hands, slick blue-grey hide, a scrap of hide at the waist, a crude stone-tipped spear; 2 a dark-elf scout with
ash-grey skin and white hair under a black hood, the face covered below the eyes by a wrapped black scarf, two long slender curved
blades of plain steel as long as his arms; 3 the same creature as the one above it, clearly upgraded and told apart at a glance: the
same face and build but larger, heavier armed, more ornate and richer in colour: still the hunched eyeless beast, darker red-brown
hide, a taller crest of spines, bone spikes tied to the forearms, a heavier barbed spear; 4 the same creature as the one above it,
clearly upgraded and told apart at a glance: the same face and build but larger, heavier armed, more ornate and richer in colour: a
close black mask hiding the whole face but the eyes, two even longer curved sword-blades of dark steel with silver inlay, no glow on
the blades, a cloak melting into black shadow
```
