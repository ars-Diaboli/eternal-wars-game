# Creature icon prompts: Necropolis

The Necropolis portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Each sheet holds one creature line, the base on the left and its
upgrade on the right. The output rules and the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative
prompt is [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

Sheet N10 holds the specter and the banshee, and sheet N11 the mummy and the lich dragon: Necropolis creatures by lore, not hired in
the town (neutral in the game), drawn in its style and on its backdrop; their files keep their neutral ids.

## Portraits

**Sheet N1** · 1024 × 768 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Strong directional light from the top left with crisp highlights and a faint cool
rim light, deep rich shadows and high contrast, a full range of values from near-black to bright highlights, rich deep natural
colours, vivid but never garish, never faded or washed out. Each portrait shows the whole creature at full height, from the top of the
head (or wings) down to the feet, a rider together with the whole mount down to its hooves, beasts, giant creatures and dragons whole
as well, in three-quarter view facing right; never cropped at the chest, waist or knees, every figure on the sheet framed the same
full-length way. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no drawn outline. Not a photograph,
not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Necropolis creatures: the dead of many ages, grey bone, rusted iron, rotting grey cloth, ash,
near black to ash grey, a cold cyan glow in eyes and wounds only; ancient and patient. 2 separate icons side by side in one row, each
alone in its own equal upright cell 2 wide by 3 tall, the whole creature fitted inside its cell from the top of the head to the feet
with about 8 % empty margin, a long or wide creature (a rider, a beast, a dragon) drawn smaller so that its whole length fits the
cell's width, both at the same scale and with the same light; nothing crosses into the other cell. No grid lines, no borders, no
frames, no text, no labels, no numbers. Transparent background, PNG with alpha. The icons, left to right: 1 a bare skeleton warrior of
faded dull grey bone, chalky and weathered, not brown or yellowed, a few rags, cold cyan light in the eye sockets, a rusted one-handed
war axe in its hand and no shield; 2 the same creature as the one to its left, clearly upgraded and told apart at a glance: the same
skull and build but larger, better armed, more ornate and richer in colour: the same faded grey bone, rusted plate pieces bolted onto
the bones, a rusted straight sword and a tall shield with a bony hand emblem, no axe
```
