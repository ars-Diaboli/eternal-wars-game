# Creature icon prompts: Necropolis

The Necropolis portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Each sheet holds two creatures in its top row and their upgrades
directly below them. The output rules and the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative
prompt is [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

## Portraits

**Sheet N1** · 1024 × 1024 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Naturalistic light from the top left with soft shadows and a faint cool fill
light, deep shadows and a full range of values, muted natural colours. Each portrait shows the creature from the chest up in
three-quarter view facing right, head and weapon inside the middle of the cell; small creatures and beasts show the whole body, giant
creatures and dragons only the head and neck. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no
drawn outline. Not a photograph, not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Necropolis creatures: the dead of many ages, grey bone,
rusted iron, rotting grey cloth, ash, near black to ash grey, a cold cyan glow in eyes and wounds only; ancient and patient. 4
separate icons arranged in a grid of 2 columns and 2 rows, each alone in its own equal square cell, centred with about 10 % empty
margin, all at the same scale and with the same light; nothing crosses into another cell. No grid lines, no borders, no frames, no
text, no labels, no numbers. Transparent background, PNG with alpha. The icons, top row left to right, then bottom row: 1 a skeleton
soldier with a rusted sword and a cracked round shield, cold cyan light in the eye sockets; 2 a hunched bloated corpse wrapped in
rags, carrying a dripping censer of cyan fumes; 3 the same creature as the one above it, upgraded, bigger and more dangerous: rusted
plate pieces bolted onto the bones, a tall shield with a bony hand emblem, a notched axe; 4 the same creature as the one above it,
upgraded, bigger and more dangerous: a hulking golem of corpses stitched together with iron staples, one enormous fist
```
