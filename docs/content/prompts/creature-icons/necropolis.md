# Creature icon prompts: Necropolis

The Necropolis portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Each sheet holds two creatures in its top row and their upgrades
directly below them. The output rules and the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative
prompt is [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

The right column of sheet N5 holds the specter and the banshee: Necropolis creatures by lore, not hired in the town (neutral
in the game), drawn in its style and on its backdrop; their files keep their neutral ids.

## Portraits

**Sheet N1** · 1024 × 1024 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Strong directional light from the top left with crisp highlights and a faint cool
rim light, deep rich shadows and high contrast, a full range of values from near-black to bright highlights, rich deep natural
colours, vivid but never garish, never faded or washed out. Each portrait shows the creature from the chest up in three-quarter view
facing right, head and weapon inside the middle of the cell; small creatures and beasts show the whole body, giant creatures and
dragons only the head and neck. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no drawn outline.
Not a photograph, not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Necropolis creatures: the dead of many ages, grey bone, rusted iron, rotting
grey cloth, ash, near black to ash grey, a cold cyan glow in eyes and wounds only; ancient and patient. 4 separate icons arranged in a
grid of 2 columns and 2 rows, each alone in its own equal square cell, centred with about 10 % empty margin, all at the same scale and
with the same light; nothing crosses into another cell. No grid lines, no borders, no frames, no text, no labels, no numbers.
Transparent background, PNG with alpha. The icons, top row left to right, then bottom row: 1 a bare skeleton warrior of faded dull
grey bone, chalky and weathered, not brown or yellowed, a few rags, cold cyan light in the eye sockets, a rusted one-handed war axe in
its hand and no shield; 2 a plain shambling zombie, a dead man risen from the grave, slack grey-green rotting skin, sunken
eyes with a faint cyan glint, torn shirt and breeches, bare feet, arms reaching forward with empty hands, no censer, no lantern, no
robe, no weapon; 3 the same creature as the one above it, clearly upgraded and told apart at a glance: the same skull and build but
larger, better armed, more ornate and richer in colour: the same faded grey bone, rusted plate pieces bolted onto the bones, a rusted
straight sword and a tall shield with a bony hand emblem, no axe; 4 the same creature as the one above it, clearly upgraded and told
apart at a glance: the same dead face but larger, heavier and more ornate: a hulking golem of corpses stitched together with iron
staples, one enormous fist
```
