# Creature icon prompts: Swamp Bestiary

The Swamp Bestiary portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Each sheet holds two creatures in its top row and their
upgrades directly below them. The output rules and the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the
negative prompt is [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

## Portraits

**Sheet W1** · 1024 × 1024 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Strong directional light from the top left with crisp highlights and a faint cool
rim light, deep rich shadows and high contrast, a full range of values from near-black to bright highlights, rich deep natural
colours, vivid but never garish, never faded or washed out. Each portrait shows the whole creature at full height, from the top of
the head (or wings) down to the feet, a rider together with the whole mount down to its hooves, beasts, giant creatures and dragons
whole as well, in three-quarter view facing right; never cropped at the chest, waist or knees, every figure on the sheet framed the
same full-length way. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no drawn outline.
Not a photograph, not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Swamp Bestiary creatures: beasts and beast-folk of the drowned pine forests,
wet scales, spiral shells, reeds, driftwood and bone, dark pine green and near-black green, pale algae-stained stone, a faint cyan
glow in eyes and markings only; feral and ancient. 4 separate icons arranged in a grid of 2 columns and 2 rows, each alone in its own
equal square cell, centred with about 10 % empty margin, all at the same scale and with the same light; nothing crosses into another
cell. No grid lines, no borders, no frames, no text, no labels, no numbers. Transparent background, PNG with alpha. The icons, top row
left to right, then bottom row: 1 a green-scaled lizardman, lean and
wiry, a skinny narrow-chested body with thin limbs and a long thin tail, not bulky or muscular, a long spear held in both hands, no
shield; 2 a small
turtle-like river creature with a shell on its back, a beak and a plain rounded scaly head with no hollow, no dish and no pool of water on it, the whole body shown; 3 the same
creature as the one above it, clearly upgraded and told apart at a glance: the same face and build but larger, heavier armed, more
ornate and richer in colour: still lean and wiry, not bulky, armour of large fish scales, the same long spear with a barbed head of
bone in one hand and a shield made of a giant shell in the other, no sword; 4 the same creature as the one above it, clearly
upgraded and told apart at a glance: the same face and build but larger, heavier armed, more ornate and richer in colour: still no
water pool on the head, a necklace of shells, glowing teal markings, a jet of water spat from its beak
```
