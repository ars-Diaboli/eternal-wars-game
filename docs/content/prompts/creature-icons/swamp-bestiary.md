# Creature icon prompts: Swamp Bestiary

The Swamp Bestiary portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Each sheet holds one creature line, the base on the left and
its upgrade on the right. The output rules and the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative
prompt is [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

Sheet W10 holds the shaman and the witch doctor: Swamp Bestiary creatures by lore, not hired in the town (neutral in the game,
ADR-361), drawn in its style and on its backdrop; their files keep their neutral ids.

## Portraits

**Sheet W1** · 1024 × 768 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Strong directional light from the top left with crisp highlights and a faint cool
rim light, deep rich shadows and high contrast, a full range of values from near-black to bright highlights, rich deep natural
colours, vivid but never garish, never faded or washed out. Each portrait shows the whole creature at full height, from the top of the
head (or wings) down to the feet, a rider together with the whole mount down to its hooves, beasts, giant creatures and dragons whole
as well, in three-quarter view facing right; never cropped at the chest, waist or knees, every figure on the sheet framed the same
full-length way. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no drawn outline. Not a photograph,
not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Swamp Bestiary creatures: beasts and beast-folk of the drowned pine forests, wet scales,
spiral shells, reeds, driftwood and bone, dark pine green and near-black green, pale algae-stained stone, a faint cyan glow in eyes
and markings only; feral and ancient. 2 separate icons side by side in one row, each alone in its own equal upright cell 2 wide by 3
tall, the whole creature fitted inside its cell from the top of the head to the feet with about 8 % empty margin, a long or wide
creature (a rider, a beast, a dragon) drawn smaller so that its whole length fits the cell's width, both at the same scale and with
the same light; nothing crosses into the other cell. No grid lines, no borders, no frames, no text, no labels, no numbers. Transparent
background, PNG with alpha. The icons, left to right: 1 a green-scaled lizardman, lean and wiry, a skinny narrow-chested body with
thin limbs and a long thin tail, not bulky or muscular, a long spear held in both hands, no shield; 2 the same creature as the one to
its left, clearly upgraded and told apart at a glance: the same face and build but larger, heavier armed, more ornate and richer in
colour: still lean and wiry, not bulky, armour of large fish scales, the same long spear with a barbed head of bone in one hand and a
shield made of a giant shell in the other, no sword
```
