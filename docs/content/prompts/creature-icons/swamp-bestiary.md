# Creature icon prompts: Swamp Bestiary

The Swamp Bestiary portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Each sheet holds three creatures in its top row and their
upgrades directly below them. The output rules and the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the
negative prompt is [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

## Portraits

**Sheet W1** · 1536 × 1024 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Naturalistic light from the top left with soft shadows and a faint cool fill
light, deep shadows and a full range of values, muted natural colours. Each portrait shows the creature from the chest up in
three-quarter view facing right, head and weapon inside the middle of the cell; small creatures and beasts show the whole body, giant
creatures and dragons only the head and neck. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no
drawn outline. Not a photograph, not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Swamp Bestiary creatures: beasts and beast-folk of the drowned
lowlands, wet scales, shells, reeds, driftwood and bone, algae and moss green, muddy brown; feral and ancient. 6 separate icons
arranged in a grid of 3 columns and 2 rows, each alone in its own equal square cell, centred with about 10 % empty margin, all at the
same scale and with the same light; nothing crosses into another cell. No grid lines, no borders, no frames, no text, no labels, no
numbers. Transparent background, PNG with alpha. The icons, top row left to right, then bottom row: 1 a green-scaled lizardman with a
short spear and a shield made of a giant shell; 2 a small turtle-like river creature with a shell on its back, a beak and a
water-filled hollow on its head, the whole body shown; 3 a lean big cat with moss-green striped fur, long fangs, swamp reeds caught in
its fur; 4 the same creature as the one above it, upgraded, bigger and more dangerous: armour of large fish scales, a sword edged with
teeth; 5 the same creature as the one above it, upgraded, bigger and more dangerous: a necklace of shells, glowing teal markings, a jet
of water spat from its beak; 6 the same creature as the one above it, upgraded, bigger and more dangerous: black stripes, bony ridges
along the spine, glowing yellow eyes
```
