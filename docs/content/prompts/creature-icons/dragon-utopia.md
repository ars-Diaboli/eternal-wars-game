# Creature icon prompts: Dragon Utopia

The Dragon Utopia portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Each sheet holds single creatures, one a cell. The output rules
and the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative prompt is
[`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

## Portraits

**Sheet U1** · 1536 × 1024 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Naturalistic light from the top left with soft shadows and a faint cool fill
light, deep shadows and a full range of values, muted natural colours. Each portrait shows the creature from the chest up in
three-quarter view facing right, head and weapon inside the middle of the cell; small creatures and beasts show the whole body, giant
creatures and dragons only the head and neck. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no
drawn outline. Not a photograph, not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Dragon Utopia creatures: the last dragons and their kin, old
and scarred, heavy scales, horns worn smooth, weathered sandstone and old bronze tones besides each dragon's own colour. 6 separate
icons arranged in a grid of 3 columns and 2 rows, each alone in its own equal square cell, centred with about 10 % empty margin, all at
the same scale and with the same light; nothing crosses into another cell. No grid lines, no borders, no frames, no text, no labels, no
numbers. Transparent background, PNG with alpha. The icons, top row left to right, then bottom row: 1 a wingless serpent-like dragon
kin with a cobra hood of bronze scales, fangs bared; 2 the same serpent-like dragon kin, larger, a golden hood crested with horns,
gem-studded scales; 3 a dragon-headed humanoid warrior with sandstone-coloured scales, a broad cleaving axe of old bronze; 4 a small
dragon with soft gold and teal butterfly wings, tiny lightning sparks on its whiskers, the whole body shown; 5 the head and neck of a
red dragon, heavy swept horns, smoke from the nostrils, embers in the jaws; 6 the head and neck of a green dragon with a frill of
spines, green vapour curling from its mouth
```
