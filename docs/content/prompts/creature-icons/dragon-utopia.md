# Creature icon prompts: Dragon Utopia

The Dragon Utopia portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Each sheet holds two creatures, one a cell; U1 holds the haspid
and its upgrade, U5 the black dragon and a second take. The output rules and the checks are in
[creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative prompt is
[`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

## Portraits

**Sheet U1** · 1024 × 768 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Strong directional light from the top left with crisp highlights and a faint cool
rim light, deep rich shadows and high contrast, a full range of values from near-black to bright highlights, rich deep natural
colours, vivid but never garish, never faded or washed out. Each portrait shows the whole creature at full height, from the top of the
head (or wings) down to the feet, a rider together with the whole mount down to its hooves, beasts, giant creatures and dragons whole
as well, in three-quarter view facing right; never cropped at the chest, waist or knees, every figure on the sheet framed the same
full-length way. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no drawn outline. Not a photograph,
not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Dragon Utopia creatures: the last dragons and their kin, old and scarred, heavy scales, horns
worn smooth, dark granite grey and old green bronze tones besides each dragon's own colour. 2 separate icons side by side in one row,
each alone in its own equal upright cell 2 wide by 3 tall, the whole creature fitted inside its cell from the top of the head to the
feet with about 8 % empty margin, a long or wide creature (a rider, a beast, a dragon) drawn smaller so that its whole length fits the
cell's width, both at the same scale and with the same light; nothing crosses into the other cell. No grid lines, no borders, no
frames, no text, no labels, no numbers. Transparent background, PNG with alpha. The icons, left to right: 1 a wingless serpent-like
dragon kin with a cobra hood of bronze scales, fangs bared; 2 the same serpent-like dragon kin, larger, a golden hood crested with
horns, gem-studded scales
```

light, a distant broken dragon statue on a pinnacle
