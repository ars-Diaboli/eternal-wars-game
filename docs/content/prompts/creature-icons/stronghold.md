# Creature icon prompts: Stronghold

The Stronghold portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Each sheet holds two creatures in its top row and their upgrades
directly below them. The output rules and the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative
prompt is [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

## Portraits

**Sheet T1** · 1024 × 1024 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Strong directional light from the top left with crisp highlights and a faint cool
rim light, deep rich shadows and high contrast, a full range of values from near-black to bright highlights, rich deep natural
colours, vivid but never garish, never faded or washed out. Each portrait shows the creature from the chest up in three-quarter view
facing right, head and weapon inside the middle of the cell; small creatures and beasts show the whole body, giant creatures and
dragons only the head and neck. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no drawn outline.
Not a photograph, not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Stronghold creatures: orc clans and the beasts of the badlands, raw hide,
rope, bone and rusted scrap iron, ochre dust, red-ochre war paint; rough and enduring. 4 separate icons arranged in a grid of 2
columns and 2 rows, each alone in its own equal square cell, centred with about 10 % empty margin, all at the same scale and with the
same light; nothing crosses into another cell. No grid lines, no borders, no frames, no text, no labels, no numbers. Transparent
background, PNG with alpha. The icons, top row left to right, then bottom row: 1 a small wiry green goblin with big ears and a sly
grin, a hide vest and a crude spear; 2 a harpy with a wild-haired human head and torso, dusty grey wings and taloned bird legs; 3 the
same creature as the one above it, clearly upgraded and told apart at a glance: the same face and build but larger, heavier armed,
more ornate and richer in colour: taller, a dented scrap-iron helmet, a spiked club, red-ochre war paint; 4 the same creature as the
one above it, clearly upgraded and told apart at a glance: the same face and build but larger, heavier armed, more ornate and richer
in colour: black wings, a red-ochre painted face, iron caps on the talons
```
