# Creature icon prompts: Stronghold

The Stronghold portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Each sheet holds one creature line, the base on the left and its
upgrade on the right. The output rules and the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative
prompt is [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

## Portraits

**Sheet T1** · 1024 × 768 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Strong directional light from the top left with crisp highlights and a faint cool
rim light, deep rich shadows and high contrast, a full range of values from near-black to bright highlights, rich deep natural
colours, vivid but never garish, never faded or washed out. Each portrait shows the whole creature at full height, from the top of the
head (or wings) down to the feet, a rider together with the whole mount down to its hooves, beasts, giant creatures and dragons whole
as well, in three-quarter view facing right; never cropped at the chest, waist or knees, every figure on the sheet framed the same
full-length way. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no drawn outline. Not a photograph,
not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Stronghold creatures: orc clans and the beasts of the badlands, raw hide, rope, bone and
rusted scrap iron, ochre dust, red-ochre war paint; rough and enduring. 2 separate icons side by side in one row, each alone in its
own equal upright cell 2 wide by 3 tall, the whole creature fitted inside its cell from the top of the head to the feet with about 8 %
empty margin, a long or wide creature (a rider, a beast, a dragon) drawn smaller so that its whole length fits the cell's width, both
at the same scale and with the same light; nothing crosses into the other cell. No grid lines, no borders, no frames, no text, no
labels, no numbers. Transparent background, PNG with alpha. The icons, left to right: 1 a small wiry green goblin with big ears and an
angry face, a furious scowl, brows knotted, teeth bared in a snarl, no smile and no grin, a hide vest and a crude spear; 2 the same
creature as the one to its left, clearly upgraded and told apart at a glance: the same face and build but larger, heavier armed, more
ornate and richer in colour: taller, the same angry snarling face, no smile, a dented scrap-iron helmet, a spiked club, red-ochre war
paint
```
