# Creature icon prompts: Stronghold

The Stronghold portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Each sheet holds three creatures in its top row and their upgrades
directly below them. The output rules and the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative
prompt is [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

## Portraits

**Sheet T1** · 1536 × 1024 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Naturalistic light from the top left with soft shadows and a faint cool fill
light, deep shadows and a full range of values, muted natural colours. Each portrait shows the creature from the chest up in
three-quarter view facing right, head and weapon inside the middle of the cell; small creatures and beasts show the whole body, giant
creatures and dragons only the head and neck. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no
drawn outline. Not a photograph, not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Stronghold creatures: orc clans and the beasts of the
badlands, raw hide, rope, bone and rusted scrap iron, dust, red-ochre war paint; rough and enduring. 6 separate icons arranged in a
grid of 3 columns and 2 rows, each alone in its own equal square cell, centred with about 10 % empty margin, all at the same scale and
with the same light; nothing crosses into another cell. No grid lines, no borders, no frames, no text, no labels, no numbers.
Transparent background, PNG with alpha. The icons, top row left to right, then bottom row: 1 a small wiry green goblin with big ears
and a sly grin, a hide vest and a crude spear; 2 a harpy with a wild-haired human head and torso, dusty grey wings and taloned bird
legs; 3 a muscular grey-green orc with tusks, a leather harness and a heavy cleaver; 4 the same creature as the one above it, upgraded,
bigger and more dangerous: taller, a dented scrap-iron helmet, a spiked club, red-ochre war paint; 5 the same creature as the one above
it, upgraded, bigger and more dangerous: black wings, a red-ochre painted face, iron caps on the talons; 6 the same creature as the one
above it, upgraded, bigger and more dangerous: a bare chest covered in red-ochre scars, an axe in each hand, a bone necklace, a furious
roar
```
