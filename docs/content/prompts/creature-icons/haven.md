# Creature icon prompts: Haven

The Haven portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Each sheet holds two creatures in its top row and their upgrades
directly below them. The output rules and the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative
prompt is [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

## Portraits

**Sheet H1** · 1024 × 1024 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Strong directional light from the top left with crisp highlights and a faint cool
rim light, deep rich shadows and high contrast, a full range of values from near-black to bright highlights, rich deep natural
colours, vivid but never garish, never faded or washed out. Each portrait shows the creature from the chest up in three-quarter view
facing right, head and weapon inside the middle of the cell; small creatures and beasts show the whole body, giant creatures and
dragons only the head and neck. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no drawn outline.
Not a photograph, not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Haven creatures: human soldiers, zealots and their beasts, grey steel and
black iron, worn brass trim, off-white cloth with a gold sun, a touch of sky blue; disciplined and devout. 4 separate icons arranged
in a grid of 2 columns and 2 rows, each alone in its own equal square cell, centred with about 10 % empty margin, all at the same
scale and with the same light; nothing crosses into another cell. No grid lines, no borders, no frames, no text, no labels, no
numbers. Transparent background, PNG with alpha. The icons, top row left to right, then bottom row: 1 a human medieval foot soldier, a
plain man-at-arms of a lord's levy: a dented steel kettle helmet, a mail shirt over a grey quilted gambeson, a single straight
arming sword of true length (a blade about as long as his arm, a cross guard, one-handed), held ready beside a round wooden shield
with an iron rim and boss; 2 a gaunt human pikeman in an open steel sallet and padded coat, a long wooden spear held upright in both
hands, its shaft running out of the cell at the bottom and its narrow steel head rising far above the helmet, the spear at least
twice his height; 3 the same creature as the one above it, clearly upgraded and told apart at a glance: the same face and build but
larger, heavier armed, more ornate and richer in colour: still one sword only, the same true-length arming sword with a brass
pommel; costly armour: a polished steel bascinet with a small brass sun on the brow, a steel breastplate and pauldrons over fine
mail, a clean off-white surcoat with a gold sun; a tall kite shield, pointed at the bottom, painted off-white with a gold sun, not
round; 4 the same creature as the one above it, clearly upgraded and told apart at a glance: the same face and build but larger,
heavier armed, more ornate and richer in colour: full steel plate with brass edges, no shield, a long halberd held in both hands, an
axe blade with a back spike and a spear point on a tall wooden haft rising above the helmet
```
