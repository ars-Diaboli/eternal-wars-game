# Creature icon prompts: Academy

The Academy portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Each sheet holds one creature line, the base on the left and its
upgrade on the right. The output rules and the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative
prompt is [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

Sheets A10 and A11 hold the Academy's creatures that are not hired in the town (neutral in the game: the thane and thunder thane, the
efreet and the golden golem), drawn in the Academy's style and on its backdrop; their files keep their neutral ids.

## Portraits

**Sheet A1** · 1024 × 768 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Strong directional light from the top left with crisp highlights and a faint cool
rim light, deep rich shadows and high contrast, a full range of values from near-black to bright highlights, rich deep natural
colours, vivid but never garish, never faded or washed out. Each portrait shows the whole creature at full height, from the top of the
head (or wings) down to the feet, a rider together with the whole mount down to its hooves, beasts, giant creatures and dragons whole
as well, in three-quarter view facing right; never cropped at the chest, waist or knees, every figure on the sheet framed the same
full-length way. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no drawn outline. Not a photograph,
not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Academy creatures: mages, dwarves, spirits and bound constructs of the frozen peaks; dark
blue-grey granite, snow and frost, bright polished steel, gold and brass trim, deep saturated royal-blue cloth with a gold star,
ice-blue rune light; no green, no teal, no verdigris anywhere; cold and scholarly. Stone, iron and spirit creatures wear no cloth. 2
separate icons side by side in one row, each alone in its own equal upright cell 2 wide by 3 tall, the whole creature fitted inside
its cell from the top of the head to the feet with about 8 % empty margin, a long or wide creature (a rider, a beast, a dragon) drawn
smaller so that its whole length fits the cell's width, both at the same scale and with the same light; nothing crosses into the other
cell. No grid lines, no borders, no frames, no text, no labels, no numbers. Transparent background, PNG with alpha. The icons, left to
right: 1 a small big-eared gremlin with grey-green skin and a toothy grin, ragged plain sackcloth, dragging an iron ball on a chain,
the whole body shown; 2 the same creature as the one to its left, clearly upgraded and told apart at a glance: the same face and build
but larger, heavier armed, more ornate and richer in colour: a crude oversized hand-made gun held at the hip, ready to fire: a short
thick barrel of riveted brass and copper bands ending in a wide flared bell-shaped muzzle like a blunderbuss, a bulbous round powder
chamber behind it, a stubby wooden stock, too big for the gremlin and patched together, not a long musket or rifle, a powder horn and
a leather bandolier of paper cartridges, a battered leather cap with a gold star badge, a royal-blue tabard; hand-made old-world
gunpowder gear, nothing sci-fi, no goggles, no tanks, no glowing devices
```
