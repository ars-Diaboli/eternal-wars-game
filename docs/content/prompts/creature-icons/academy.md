# Creature icon prompts: Academy

The Academy portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Each sheet holds two creatures in its top row and their upgrades
directly below them. The output rules and the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative
prompt is [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

Sheet A6 holds the Academy's creatures that are not hired in the town (neutral in the game: the thane and thunder thane, the efreet
and the golden golem), drawn in the Academy's style and on its backdrop; their files keep their neutral ids.

## Portraits

**Sheet A1** · 1024 × 1024 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Strong directional light from the top left with crisp highlights and a faint cool
rim light, deep rich shadows and high contrast, a full range of values from near-black to bright highlights, rich deep natural
colours, vivid but never garish, never faded or washed out. Each portrait shows the creature from the chest up in three-quarter view
facing right, head and weapon inside the middle of the cell; small creatures and beasts show the whole body, giant creatures and
dragons only the head and neck. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no drawn outline.
Not a photograph, not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Academy creatures: mages, dwarves, spirits and bound constructs of the
frozen peaks; dark blue-grey granite, snow and frost, bright polished steel, gold and brass trim, deep saturated royal-blue cloth with
a gold star, ice-blue rune light; no green, no teal, no verdigris anywhere; cold and scholarly. Stone, iron and spirit creatures wear
no cloth. 4 separate icons arranged in a grid of 2 columns and 2 rows, each alone in its own equal square cell, centred with about 10
% empty margin, all at the same scale and with the same light; nothing crosses into another cell. No grid lines, no borders, no
frames, no text, no labels, no numbers. Transparent background, PNG with alpha. The icons, top row left to right, then bottom row: 1 a
small big-eared gremlin with grey-green skin and a toothy grin, ragged plain sackcloth, dragging an iron ball on a chain, the whole
body shown; 2 a stocky dwarf in a plain dented iron helmet, a padded leather coat and a grey fur cloak, a braided red beard, a plain
iron war hammer; 3 the same creature as the one above it, clearly upgraded and told apart at a glance: the same face and build but
larger, heavier armed, more ornate and richer in colour: a long old flintlock musket nearly as long as its body held ready to fire, a
powder horn and a leather bandolier of paper cartridges, a battered leather cap with a gold star badge, a royal-blue tabard; hand-made
old-world gunpowder gear, nothing sci-fi, no goggles, no tanks, no glowing devices; 4 the same creature as the one above it, clearly
upgraded and told apart at a glance: the same face and build but larger, heavier armed, more ornate and richer in colour: full heavy
plate of bright polished steel with gold trim, a closed steel helm with a gold crest, a large square steel shield with a gold star, a
royal-blue surcoat, the beard braided with gold rings
```
