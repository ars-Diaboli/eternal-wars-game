# Creature icon prompts: Sylvan

The Sylvan portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Each sheet holds one creature line, the base on the left and its
upgrade on the right. The output rules and the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative
prompt is [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

Sheet S10 holds the timber wolf and the dire wolf: Sylvan creatures by lore, not hired in the town (neutral in the game), drawn in its
style and on its backdrop; their files keep their neutral ids.

## Portraits

**Sheet S1** · 1024 × 768 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Strong directional light from the top left with crisp highlights and a faint cool
rim light, deep rich shadows and high contrast, a full range of values from near-black to bright highlights, rich deep natural
colours, vivid but never garish, never faded or washed out. Each portrait shows the whole creature at full height, from the top of the
head (or wings) down to the feet, a rider together with the whole mount down to its hooves, beasts, giant creatures and dragons whole
as well, in three-quarter view facing right; never cropped at the chest, waist or knees, every figure on the sheet framed the same
full-length way. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no drawn outline. Not a photograph,
not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Sylvan creatures: elves, fey and beasts of an ancient misty forest; elves in fitted leather
and pale carved-wood armour with leaf-shaped steel and gold-leaf trim, deep forest green, grey-brown and silver, a faint pale-green
glow; graceful and proud. Leaves, moss and vines appear only as patterns on clothing and armour, never growing on bodies, fur or
weapons. 2 separate icons side by side in one row, each alone in its own equal upright cell 2 wide by 3 tall, the whole creature
fitted inside its cell from the top of the head to the feet with about 8 % empty margin, a long or wide creature (a rider, a beast, a
dragon) drawn smaller so that its whole length fits the cell's width, both at the same scale and with the same light; nothing crosses
into the other cell. No grid lines, no borders, no frames, no text, no labels, no numbers. Transparent background, PNG with alpha. The
icons, left to right: 1 a tiny faerie, a slender elven woman with pointed ears and a delicate fine-featured face, long fair hair, a
short gown of pale petals, large bright translucent dragonfly wings shimmering green and gold, a pale-green glow around her, open
empty hands and no weapon, the whole body shown; not a goblin, not grotesque; 2 the same creature as the one to its left, clearly
upgraded and told apart at a glance: the same face and build but larger, heavier armed, more ornate and richer in colour: large
luminous butterfly wings of bright blue, violet and gold, a crown of tiny white flowers, a silver gown, a bright silver-green glow,
still no weapon
```
