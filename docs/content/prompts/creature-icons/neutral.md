# Creature icon prompts: Neutral

The Neutral portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole: X1 the peasant and the halfling, X2 the gnoll and the bandit, X3 the
troll and the mammoth. Most wandering creatures belong to a town by lore and are drawn on its sheets: the thanes, the efreet and the
golden golem in [academy.md](academy.md) (A10, A11), the specter, the banshee, the mummy and the lich dragon in
[necropolis.md](necropolis.md) (N10, N11), the timber and dire wolves in [sylvan.md](sylvan.md) (S10), the gogs and the breeders in
[inferno.md](inferno.md) (I10, I11), the shaman and the witch doctor in [swamp-bestiary.md](swamp-bestiary.md) (W10). The output rules
and the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative prompt is
[`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

## Portraits

**Sheet X1** · 1024 × 768 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Strong directional light from the top left with crisp highlights and a faint cool
rim light, deep rich shadows and high contrast, a full range of values from near-black to bright highlights, rich deep natural
colours, vivid but never garish, never faded or washed out. Each portrait shows the whole creature at full height, from the top of the
head (or wings) down to the feet, a rider together with the whole mount down to its hooves, beasts, giant creatures and dragons whole
as well, in three-quarter view facing right; never cropped at the chest, waist or knees, every figure on the sheet framed the same
full-length way. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no drawn outline. Not a photograph,
not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Neutral creatures of the wilds and the ruins: no faction colours, natural and weathered
materials, each creature in its own colours. 2 separate icons side by side in one row, each alone in its own equal upright cell 2 wide
by 3 tall, the whole creature fitted inside its cell from the top of the head to the feet with about 8 % empty margin, a long or wide
creature (a rider, a beast, a dragon) drawn smaller so that its whole length fits the cell's width, both at the same scale and with
the same light; nothing crosses into the other cell. No grid lines, no borders, no frames, no text, no labels, no numbers. Transparent
background, PNG with alpha. The icons, left to right: 1 a medieval peasant, a gaunt weathered serf with a grim hungry face, unshaven,
a patched brown wool tunic belted with rope, a ragged grey hood, legs wrapped in muddy rags and worn leather shoes, an old wooden
pitchfork with iron tines held in both hands; 2 a halfling, a small stocky man half the height of a human, grown and weathered, not a
child, a wary grim round face with a stubbled jaw, curly brown hair, big bare hairy feet, a patched green-brown jerkin, a leather belt
with pouches, a leather sling whirling above his head in one hand, a pouch of sling stones at the hip
```
