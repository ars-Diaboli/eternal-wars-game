# Creature icon prompts: Neutral

The Neutral portraits, each sheet's prompt ready to copy whole. Sheets X1–X2 hold three creatures in the top row and their upgrades
directly below them; sheet X3 holds four single creatures. The output rules and the checks are in
[creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative prompt is
[`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

## Portraits

**Sheet X1** · 1536 × 1024 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Naturalistic light from the top left with soft shadows and a faint cool fill
light, deep shadows and a full range of values, muted natural colours. Each portrait shows the creature from the chest up in
three-quarter view facing right, head and weapon inside the middle of the cell; small creatures and beasts show the whole body, giant
creatures and dragons only the head and neck. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no
drawn outline. Not a photograph, not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Neutral creatures of the wilds and the ruins: no faction
colours, natural and weathered materials, each creature in its own colours. 6 separate icons arranged in a grid of 3 columns and 2
rows, each alone in its own equal square cell, centred with about 10 % empty margin, all at the same scale and with the same light;
nothing crosses into another cell. No grid lines, no borders, no frames, no text, no labels, no numbers. Transparent background, PNG
with alpha. The icons, top row left to right, then bottom row: 1 a grey timber wolf with raised hackles, snarling, the whole body
shown; 2 a drifting pale-grey ghost in a torn hooded shroud, an empty dark face; 3 the head and shoulders of a mountain giant with
frost-grey skin and a braided beard, a huge stone axe; 4 the same creature as the one above it, upgraded, bigger and more dangerous:
huge and scarred, a ridge of bristling black fur; 5 the same creature as the one above it, upgraded, bigger and more dangerous: long
white hair streaming upward, the mouth wide open in a wail; 6 the same creature as the one above it, upgraded, bigger and more
dangerous: lightning crackling in the beard, an iron storm crown, a hammer wrapped in lightning
```
