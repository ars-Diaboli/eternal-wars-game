# Creature icon prompts: Neutral

The Neutral portraits, the sheet's prompt ready to copy whole: the mammoth over its upgrade in the left column, the bandit over the
troll in the right. Most wandering creatures belong to a town by lore and are drawn on its sheets: the thanes, the efreet and the
golden golem in [academy.md](academy.md) (A6), the specter and the banshee in [necropolis.md](necropolis.md) (N5), the timber and
dire wolves in [sylvan.md](sylvan.md) (S5), the gogs and the breeders in [inferno.md](inferno.md) (I5, I6). The output rules and
the checks are in [creature-icon-prompts.md](../creature-icon-prompts.md); the negative prompt is
[`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt) there.

## Portraits

**Sheet X1** · 1024 × 1024 · negative [`NEG-CREATURE`](../creature-icon-prompts.md#negative-prompt)

```text
Game creature portrait sheet in a realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, like a detailed digital oil painting or a dark-fantasy book
illustration: believable anatomy and proportions, real worn materials with fine texture (dented and rusted armour, stained cloth,
scarred skin, matted fur, cracked scales, wet hide). Strong directional light from the top left with crisp highlights and a faint cool
rim light, deep rich shadows and high contrast, a full range of values from near-black to bright highlights, rich deep natural
colours, vivid but never garish, never faded or washed out. Each portrait shows the creature from the chest up in three-quarter view
facing right, head and weapon inside the middle of the cell; small creatures and beasts show the whole body, giant creatures and
dragons only the head and neck. Grim, weathered and menacing, never cute; the figure set apart by value contrast, no drawn outline.
Not a photograph, not a plastic CGI render, no pixel art. Neutral creatures of the wilds and the ruins: no faction colours, natural
and weathered materials, each creature in its own colours. 4 separate icons arranged in a grid of 2 columns and 2 rows, each alone in
its own equal square cell, centred with about 10 % empty margin, all at the same scale and with the same light; nothing crosses into
another cell. No grid lines, no borders, no frames, no text, no labels, no numbers. Transparent background, PNG with alpha. The icons,
top row left to right, then bottom row: 1 a woolly mammoth with long curved tusks and shaggy dark fur; 2 a hooded human bandit with a
scarf over the lower face, a short sword and a dagger; 3 the same creature as the one above it, clearly upgraded and told apart at a
glance: the same face and build but larger, heavier armed, more ornate and richer in colour: a wooden fighting tower on its back, iron
caps on the tusks; 4 a tall green-grey troll with long arms and mossy hide, a branch club, wounds closing on its skin
```
