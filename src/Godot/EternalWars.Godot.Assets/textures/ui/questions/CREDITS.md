# Question pictures

The pictures on the adventure map's question cards (`EternalWars.Godot.UI.Hud.DecisionPanel`, chosen by `QuestionPictures`), one per
kind of question, `q_<id>.png`. Images generated for the owner from the prompts in
[question-picture-prompts.md](../../../../../../docs/content/prompts/question-picture-prompts.md) (2026-11-26); 1672 × 941, scaled to
1024 × 576 with the outer 6 % blended into the card's slate (RGB 28, 31, 38), saved as PNG.

All of the prompts' pictures but `q_event-message`, which is not generated yet; its question shows the slate placeholder.
