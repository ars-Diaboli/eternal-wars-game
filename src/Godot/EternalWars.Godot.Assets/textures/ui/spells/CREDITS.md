# Spell icons

Painted spell icons, 128 × 128, square tiles with their painted backdrop (the sheets' glow is part of each picture), generated for this
project by the owner (2026-11-21) from the sheets of [spell-icon-prompts.md](../../../../../../docs/content/prompts/spell-icon-prompts.md),
six spells a school, and cut one cell per file. Loaded through `EternalWars.Godot.UI.SpellIcons`; a spell without a file shows its name
alone.

| File | Used for |
|---|---|
| &lt;spell id&gt;.png (`light.bless.png` … `shadow.dimension-door.png`) | the spell buttons of the spell book, the hero screen and the battle, and the map editor's spell pickers |
